Probabilistic Disease Surveillance (PDS) Demonstration

A PDS system comprises tools that preprocess EMR data (e.g., Metamap Lite), detect cases of disease from EMR data such as the case detection system (CDS) and ILI Tracker which performs detection and characterization of outbreaks from the disease case likelihoods.

This interface shows live logs and ILI Tracker output images from such a system. The pipeline is implemented as a series of inbox, outbox and archive folders for each component with a file watcher that runs the components when a file is detected in its inbox.

We simulated data in this demonstration by asking ChatGPT to output ED reports for respiratory diseases, heart attack and automobile crashes. We then selected these reports randomly with replacement to generate 60 days of encounters with a mean of 20 and a std. dev. of 2 on which we overlayed day of week effects and a triangular distribution of COVID-19 patients beginning on day 45, lasting 14 days and peaking with an additional 40 patients. Note that this demonstration does not include any simulated structured data.

Pressing the "Copy ED Reports" button copies simulated ED reports from the metamap_archive folder to the metamap_inbox folder.

After copying the ED reports, the system will process the data (Metamap Lite, Brat2Csv, CDS) and then run ILI Tracker every minute. You can view the output of ILI Tracker by clicking on the graphs at the bottom of the page. It will take an hour to run through all of the reports

Pressing the Reset button will reset the system, i.e., delete reports from the Metamap Lite inbox and remove working data from the ILI tracker working directory.
